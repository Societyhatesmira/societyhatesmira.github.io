About Us

We Are More Than Construction — We Build Landmarks That Enhance Lives

Dutum Company Limited was incorporated under the laws of the Federal Republic of Nigeria in 1989. We have been among the top-rated Construction Companies in South West and Central parts of Nigeria. Managing Director Temitope Runsewe leads this charge — first-class degree in Computing, MSc Information Systems from LSE, former JP Morgan Chase London.

Our Values: Safety First, Client First, Excellence, Commitment to Sustainability, Expertise, Dedicated Team. We work in partnership with sophisticated customers who value the highest levels of quality, safety and technical expertise.
