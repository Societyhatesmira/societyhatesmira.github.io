About — Operating across all Nigerian states

We create and care for essential assets.

Dutum Company Limited, fully incorporated in 1989 under Nigeria's laws. For over three decades, we have built hospitals, bridges, schools, roads, petrol stations, luxury estates and industrial facilities.

We offer traditional contract-based services as well as full partnering agreements. Our values are safety and client satisfaction, reflected in our track record of diverse projects for government and private investors.

Business Units — 5 Divisions
01 — CIVIL

Civil Engineering

Design, construction and maintenance of physical and naturally built environment — roads, bridges, canals, dams, airports, sewerage systems, pipelines, railways.

02 — BUILDING

Building Construction

Specialty addition of structures to real property, considering cost, lifetime, safety regulations and potential hazards. On schedule, within budget.

03 — DESIGN & BUILD

Design & Build

End-to-end solutions across rural and urban Nigeria — from concept and design through construction and delivery for residential, commercial, mixed-use.

04 — EPC

Engineering, Procurement & Construction

Single point responsibility — design, procurement, construction, commissioning and handover. Ensuring adherence to schedules and budgets.

05 — FACILITY

Facility Management

Integrating people, place, process and technology to ensure built environment is functional, comfortable, safe and efficient. Three plans: DFM-Flexi, Max, Core.
